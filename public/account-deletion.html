GHOST PROTOCOL — Account Deletion

This page explains how users of GHOST PROTOCOL can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the email application you use.
  2. Send an email to ghostprotocolatk@gmail.com .
  3. Use the subject line: GHOST PROTOCOL Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include the email address associated with your GHOST PROTOCOL account so that we can identify and verify your request.
  5. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
Request Account Deletion

What data is covered?

Once a request is verified and processed, the deletion process is intended to cover the user's Firebase Authentication account and associated personal data stored in GHOST PROTOCOL's Firestore database, including profile information and submitted records where applicable.

Data held in other services, backups, or security logs must also be addressed as applicable. Any information retained for a legitimate legal or security purpose, the reason for retention, and its applicable retention period will be explained to the user.

Deletion of other data

If you want to request deletion of specific personal data without deleting your entire account, email us using the address above and describe your request.

Processing and confirmation

We will review verified requests, explain any necessary additional steps, and communicate the expected processing timeframe. We will confirm when the request has been completed or explain any applicable limitation.
