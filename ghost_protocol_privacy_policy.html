Privacy Policy for GHOST PROTOCOL

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GHOST PROTOCOL (“the app”) handles information. It is written for the app version and features described below. This policy must be updated before release whenever the app’s actual data handling or third-party integrations change.

Release-status note: Firebase authentication and Firebase database services are planned but are not implemented in the version described for this policy. Do not describe Firebase as active unless it is added to the app. Maps and emergency location sharing are intended features; the location sections below apply only when those features are implemented and used.

1. About the app

GHOST PROTOCOL is an application for motorcycle riders. Its intended features include motorcycle/garage information, reporting bike-related problems, charging-location discovery, trip planning, and rider-community assistance. Availability of individual features depends on the version installed.

2. Information handled by the current version

The information the app handles depends on the features actually available in the installed version. Information that a user enters into a feature may include motorcycle details, garage entries, technical problem reports, modifications, trip details, charging-station reports, and community posts.

Before publishing, the developer must verify where each item is stored (for example, only on the device or on a server) and whether any analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, authentication, mapping, or other third-party SDKs collect information. This policy does not claim that such services are present unless they are actually integrated.

3. Firebase and online accounts

Firebase authentication and database services are planned for a future version and are not implemented in the version covered by this policy. Therefore, this policy does not state that the current version sends information to Firebase.

If Firebase or another online service is added in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be reviewed and updated to accurately identify the data processed, the purposes, the service providers, retention, and available deletion options before that version is released.

4. Maps and location

Maps and charging-location discovery are intended features. If the released version requests device location, location access should be used only for the feature explained to the user and according to the permission granted through Android.

Users can manage location permissions in Android settings. A map may also allow users to search for or select a location manually without granting device-location permission, depending on the implementation.

The app must not be described as collecting or transmitting precise location unless the released implementation actually does so. Before release, the developer must verify whether the map provider or any SDK receives location, search queries, or other data and update this policy accordingly.

5. Emergency location sharing

Emergency location sharing is an intended feature, not a promise that assistance is continuously monitored or guaranteed. If implemented, the app should clearly explain what information will be sent, who will receive it, and when it will be sent before the user confirms the action.

This policy does not claim that emergency location is currently transmitted to community members, administrators, or any other recipient. Those details must be added and verified if the feature is implemented. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency services directly.

6. How information may be used

Depending on the features enabled in the released version, information supplied by users may be used to provide app functions, display motorcycle or charging information, organize user-submitted reports, support trip planning, respond to requests, and protect the app against misuse. The developer must ensure these purposes match the actual implementation.

7. Sharing and third-party services

This policy does not assert that the current version shares personal information with a particular third party. If a released feature sends information to a map provider, hosting provider, authentication service, database provider, or other third party, the policy must identify the relevant categories of information, purposes, and recipients.

Content that users choose to submit to a community feature may be visible to other users if that feature is implemented as a shared community. The app should explain the visibility of a submission before it is posted.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information stored only on a device may be removed when the user deletes it in the app, clears the app’s storage, or uninstalls the app; exact behavior depends on the implementation and device backups. Uninstalling an app does not necessarily remove information previously sent to an online service.

Before release, the developer must document how users can request deletion of any personal information held by the developer or its service providers and how long information is retained. If account creation is introduced, an appropriate account-deletion process must also be provided where required by Google Play policy.

9. Security

The developer should use reasonable, appropriate safeguards for information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Any security claims made about the released app should reflect safeguards that are actually implemented.

10. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, contact the developer using the contact method below.

11. Your choices

Depending on the features available, users may be able to manage Android permissions, choose what information they submit, remove locally stored entries, and contact the developer with privacy questions or deletion requests. Available controls must be confirmed against the released app.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this webpage with a revised “Last updated” date.
